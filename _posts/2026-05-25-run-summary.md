## PHT Watch (15 days post-flare)

This is the most encouraging session yet in the rehab block. A 16 km MLR at 5:04/km average with a sub-4:10 closing kilometre is a meaningful jump in both volume and intensity — and the data shows it was absorbed cleanly. Cadence stayed at 177 spm average (the protective range), and even at peak speed the acceleration was cadence-led (193 spm) rather than over-striding. No pace decay, no asymmetry in the splits, no HR spikes.

That said, this is the upper edge of what the proximal hamstring should be asked to do right now. Tomorrow should be **easy or rest** — and definitely no fast finishes back-to-back. Keep the isometric work (long-lever bridges, single-leg deadlift holds, 30-45s × 3-4) going, and continue avoiding deep hamstring stretches.

A confidence-building day: marathon-shape engine is intact, the legs are progressing, and the hamstring tolerated a real workload without protest. 👟
