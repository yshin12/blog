## Notes

- **PHT (10 days post-flare):** Both sessions stayed aerobic — peak HR 152 in the surge, no sustained hard efforts. 17 km cumulative is a respectable load, but the controlled effort and short rep duration keep proximal tendon stress modest. Watch for any glute tightness overnight; if quiet, this confirms the tissue is tolerating progressive volume well. Keep up the isometrics.
- Cadence in the 170s today (vs usual 180+) reflects the slower warm-up/recovery share; the progression's final km hit 182+ spm — good mechanics under fatigue.
- Calorie burn of 1,244 kcal is a solid stimulus — refuel with carbs + 25-30 g protein within the hour to support tendon repair.

Quietly excellent double day. 👏
