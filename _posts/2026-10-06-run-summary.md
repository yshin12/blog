### Notes

- **Good:** both goal-pace miles had race-quality form, with cadence around 190, a long stride, and no fade in mechanics. HR came down quickly afterwards (166 → 145 → 137). The progressive warm-up got you properly ready.
- **Watch:** mile 1 at 6:29 /mi (4:02 /km) was LT pace, not race pace. Fresh, tapered legs will try the same thing on Sunday, so hold back. HR was about 5–8 bpm higher than your history at this pace. The strides were close to sprints (cadence 217–221). They are the likely source of a new, slight left-groin discomfort.
- **Hamstring/glute:** it didn't limit you, and stride length held well. Keep doing the isometric bridge holds through Thursday.
