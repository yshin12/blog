## How it broke down

**Steady block (km 1–10.3) — 5:18 /km (8:32 /mi) average.** A textbook easy-to-aerobic
progression: opened at 5:33 /km (8:56 /mi), settled into the 5:16–5:21 /km (8:29–8:37 /mi)
range, then drifted down to 5:14, 5:13 and 5:09 /km (8:26, 8:24, 8:17 /mi) over the last three
kilometres. Heart rate tracked it honestly — 114 bpm on the opening kilometre, 130 bpm on the
tenth — averaging around 124 bpm through the block. That is the bottom half of the general
aerobic band (70–81 % max, 123–143 bpm), so this was a genuinely conservative aerobic run rather
than a creeping steady effort.

**Strides (last ~2.3 km) — 10 × ~90 m.** Every rep landed between 3:10 and 3:31 /km
(5:06–5:39 /mi), 15–18 seconds each, with roughly 50-second jog float recoveries at 6:12–7:11 /km
(9:59–11:34 /mi). Cadence spiked to 201–212 spm on the reps with stride length stretching to
1.78 m, and the fastest touch of the day was 2:41 /km (4:19 /mi). HR climbed the way it should
across a stride set — 116 bpm on the first rep up to 157 bpm by the eighth — and topped out at
167 bpm without ever tipping into a workout.
