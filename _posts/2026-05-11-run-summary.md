### Notes

A textbook Monday easy run to open the new week, eight days after the Toronto HM PB. Pace started conservatively in the 5:54s, drifted down to 5:22 by km 9, then closed with a relaxed 6:01 cool-down — a natural negative-split shape with HR capped at 128 in the fastest kilometre and averaging 121 bpm across the session. Cadence held an excellent 176 spm overall (179 at peak), and stride length pushed close to 1.04 m mid-run without any wobble. Effort was unmistakably aerobic the entire time.

PHT showed no warning signs today: stride length opened up cleanly during the faster kilometres, cadence stayed high, and HR did not drift upward at constant pace — all good indicators the proximal tendon is tolerating last week's 63 km load. Closing the run with a deliberate cool-down kilometre rather than a hard finish was a smart, tendon-friendly call. **Next:** keep the rhythm light — an easy 10–12 km or a true recovery jog tomorrow, then a small quality session later in the week as the body continues to absorb the HM.
