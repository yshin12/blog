### Classification and plan adherence

- **Prescribed (Pfitz transition week, Sat):** General aerobic 13 km with 2 × 6 × 100 m strides.
- **Actual:** 10 km of general aerobic running at 5:24 /km and HR ~128 (the low end of the 123–143 GA zone), then 5 strides. That is 11.3 km in total.
- The run type matched the plan, at a slightly lower volume, which suits a first run back after four days off.

### What went well

- **Patient progression.** The run started at 5:46, settled at 5:19–5:23, and the 5:15 last kilometre was the fastest of the GA portion.
- **Aerobic fitness held.** HR stayed at 117–133 bpm at about 5:20 /km, much like the Oct 17 GA run, so the rest days cost no visible fitness.
- **Stable mechanics.** Cadence held at 183–186 spm, and stride length grew late in the run rather than shortening. That is a good sign for the left hamstring/glute.

### To watch

- **Strides were too fast.** They averaged about 19 s per 100 m against the 20–22 s target, and one was about 17 s. Aim for 21 s at a "smooth 90%" effort, because the groin strain started on Oct 6 with near-sprint strides.
- **Recoveries were too short.** HR climbed from 124 to 160 bpm across the set. Take 60–90 s walk/jog recoveries so HR drops below ~130 before each stride.
