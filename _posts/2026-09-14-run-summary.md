Peak week started with an easy evening recovery run in Burlington at 18:54, the day
after a hot 26 km (16.3 mi) progression long run. The plan's Monday is **rest or
cross-training**, and a 6–8 km (4–5 mi) recovery jog also counts as on-plan. At
8.96 km (5.57 mi), this ran slightly past that range, but at a truly easy effort.

**Conditions:** 20.0 °C (68 °F), feels like 20.0 °C (68 °F), dew point 10.0 °C
(50 °F), 53 % humidity, wind 13 km/h (8 mph) from the E, Burlington Piers.
Temperature plus dew point was 118 °F, so the heat had no real effect on pace. These
were the most comfortable conditions in days.

This was a well-run recovery session. Pace stayed in the middle of the recovery band,
5:25–5:55 /km (8:45–9:30 /mi), and from mile 2 on, every split was within 2 seconds
of the others. HR held at 120–122 bpm, about 68 % of max and well under the 76 %
recovery ceiling. It never passed 128 bpm, even on the small climbs. Unlike
yesterday's cool-down, there was no leftover heat or fatigue drift. After a slow
first mile at 172 spm, cadence settled at 181–185 spm, and stride length stayed a
steady 0.95–0.97 m. There was no uneven stride or late shortening, so the data shows
nothing pointing to the left hamstring or glute.
