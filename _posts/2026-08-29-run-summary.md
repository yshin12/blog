**Run type: General aerobic (AR) + speed.** The first 8 km sat between 5:41 and 5:08 /km
(9:09–8:16 /mi) with HR climbing gently from 100 to 129 bpm — squarely in the aerobic band and
never above ~76% max. After a short jog segment, three 100 m strides came in at 3:28, 3:35 and
3:31 /km (5:35–5:46 /mi) at 190–194 spm cadence, right in the target stride range, with float
recoveries in between.

### What went well

- **Textbook aerobic control.** Avg HR 122 bpm over an easy 5:22 /km — a genuinely easy effort,
  exactly what a general aerobic day should feel like.
- **Negative-drifting pace.** Splits progressed from 5:41 down to 5:08–5:15 /km through the run
  without HR spiking, a good sign of aerobic fitness.
- **Sharp strides.** All three accelerations hit 3:28–3:35 /km with cadence over 190 spm and
  stride length up to 1.54 m — fast but relaxed, not straining.

### Plan adherence

Pfitzinger HM Schedule 2, 6 weeks to goal: today prescribed **general aerobic + speed, 13 km
(8 mi), 6 × 12 s uphill followed by 8 × 100 m strides**. Actual was 8.92 km with 3 strides and no
hill reps — about 4 km short and a reduced speed set. Reasonable trim given the left
hamstring/glute niggle: uphill 12 s bursts are the highest-load item on the menu for that tendon,
and skipping them in favour of a few flat strides is the sensible order of modification.

### Injury note

Nothing in the data suggests strain — cadence held 170+ through the aerobic portion, stride length
was symmetric-looking and stable, and the strides showed no pace fade. Keep the isometric
hamstring holds going and avoid aggressive stretching of the sit-bone region.

### Tomorrow

through the run. Given today's short volume, the legs should be fresh for it — start at the slow
