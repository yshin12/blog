### Day as a Whole

A smart, well-bracketed running day: a steady aerobic 10 km in the main session followed by a short easy shakeout for ~14 km total. Combined load (1:07:27, 890 kcal, avg HR ~123 bpm) is firmly in aerobic-development territory — no anaerobic stress, no neuromuscular load, just volume at conversational intensity. This is exactly the right shape for the current block: rebuilding consistent mileage 11 days post-HM PB and 4 days post-PHT flare without provoking the proximal hamstring.

PHT held up well. Both runs favored steady cadence, moderate stride length (1.07–1.10 m, not over-striding), and stayed below the HR ceiling where compressive hip-flexion loads tend to climb. No fast acceleration phases, no hill repeats, and no end-of-run surges — all good choices for the tendon. **Next:** keep tomorrow easy or take a full rest day if any glute signal appears, then consider an MLR or aerobic progression on the weekend.
