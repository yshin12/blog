One run today: 6.10 km on a nearly flat route in Burlington. After Saturday's 19:49 parkrun and Sunday's hilly 17.72 km, the goal was simply to keep it short and truly easy.

The plan was a rest day or 6–8 km at 5:25–5:55 /km with HR under 134, so this was on plan. The 6:08 average was slower than that band, which is fine because the pace is a ceiling, not a target. Even the 130 peak stayed under the HR limit.

The run eased along on its own: the first three km averaged 6:22 /km and the last three 5:55 /km, with HR rising only slowly. Cadence and stride were well below Sunday's 184 spm and 1.09 m, mostly because of the slower pace. I'll look at cadence again on tomorrow's run to see whether my legs are still tired.

With only 17 m of climbing, this also gave my left hamstring/glute a break from yesterday's 303 m. I still need to check it for soreness, using a firm chair, a single-leg bridge and stairs.
