Thursday was a double in Burlington, with a track session in the late afternoon and a road run in the evening. It was warm for October. The afternoon was breezy, and the evening was cooler but close to saturated.

Temperature plus dew point was 132 for both runs. That's below the range where heat costs a lot of pace, but it still means about 2–3% slower than in cool air. The evening's 88% humidity and lack of wind added some HR drift.

## 1. Afternoon (16:18): Track session

**Run type: Easy intervals (4 × 800 m at general-aerobic effort, with walk/jog recoveries).** The reps came in at 4:09–4:20 each, with HR steady at 125–126 bpm. That's an aerobic effort, not the VO2max pace the plan called for.

## 2. Evening (18:37): General aerobic run

**Run type: General aerobic (AR).** It averaged 8:29 /mi (5:16 /km) at 140 bpm, which is inside the AR zone of 123–143 bpm. Adjusted for heat, the AR band shifts to about 8:13–9:18 /mi (5:06–5:47 /km), and this run sat in the middle of it.

- **Load:** This was a moderate aerobic day. The track session was rhythm work at easy HR, and most of the day's load came from the evening AR run. You head into the last weekend of the build fresh rather than tired, which is a reasonable trade 10 days before the race.
- **Plan adherence:** This is Pfitzinger HM Schedule 2, **1 week to goal** (43 mi / 69 km). Thursday calls for **VO2max, 10 mi (16 km): 2 × 1200 m + 4 × 800 m at 6:02–6:18 /mi (3:45–3:55 /km)**. You hit the volume (10.18 mi / 16.39 km). However, the 800s were run at AR pace and the 1200s were skipped, so the VO2max stimulus didn't happen. Don't try to make it up. Saturday's strides and race week's 2 mi at HM pace will give you the sharpness you need.
- **Volume:** This week (Mon Sep 28 – Sun Oct 4) is at 30.96 mi (49.82 km) of the 43 mi (69 km) planned. October to date is 10.18 mi (16.39 km).
- **Hamstring / glute:** Today was low-risk for the tendon. There was no high-speed running, and the even 1.00 m stride on the reps showed no overstriding. The one thing to check is the 155 bpm spike with a 6:18 /mi (3:55 /km) burst in the track cool-down. That's fine if it was a stride, but worth noting if something twinged.
