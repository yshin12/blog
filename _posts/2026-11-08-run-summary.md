The two loops were very even. The descents took the same time, and the second climb was 16 s faster at the same peak HR. Climbing form was sensible, with stride shortening to 0.97–1.03 m while cadence held at 175–179 spm. The climbs did take HR to 143–154 bpm, above the 143 bpm ceiling for easy days, and the route was well beyond the planned 14–16 km flat.

Today brings the week to **71.19 km** (Mon 6.10, Tue 14.16, Wed 0, Thu 12.32, Fri 7.06, Sat 11.29, Sun 20.26), up 15% from last week's 61.98 km.

**Left hamstring/glute:** pain was 3/10 at the start of the hill run, rose to 5–6/10 later, and there was notable discomfort afterwards. That is well past the 2/10 threshold, so this is now an active flare of the proximal hamstring tendinopathy. Uphill running loads the tendon in deep hip flexion, and the long descents add braking work. The stretch since Nov 1 also stacked two hill days, a track session and strides with rising volume.

**Next:** no running Nov 9–11, with isometric bridges or holds twice a day and no hamstring stretching. Thursday Nov 12 is a 3–4 km flat test run, very easy. If pain stays at 1/10 or less, the rebuild starts with a cap of 8 km per run and 40 km for the week, and no tempo, intervals or hills.
