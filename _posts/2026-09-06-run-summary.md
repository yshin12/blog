heat-penalty range. Effectively neutral conditions with a small humidity tax; the
NE breeze off the lake would have helped on the return leg.

### Splits and shape

A textbook progression. The first 9 km averaged **5:17 /km (8:31 /mi)** and the
final 9.01 km averaged **4:51 /km (7:48 /mi)** — a 26 s/km negative split, with
every kilometre from 10 onward faster than the opening block. Km 1 opened
conservatively at 5:40 /km (9:07 /mi) and the run closed with 4:33, 4:40 /km
(7:19, 7:31 /mi).

HR tracked the effort beautifully: 111 bpm on the opening kilometre, drifting to
143–144 bpm over the last two — still comfortably aerobic at a 151 bpm max. Cadence
climbed with pace from 165 to 185 spm and stride length grew from 1.06 m to 1.17 m,
which is the right way to get faster: turnover first, then length.

### Plan adherence

Week 5 to goal (Aug 31 – Sep 6) prescribed a **progression long run of 26 km (16.2 mi)
with the last 5 km (3 mi) at LT pace (4:03–4:09 /km / 6:31–6:41 /mi)**. Actual: 18.01 km
(11.19 mi) with a strong progression that finished at endurance-to-steady pace rather
than true LT. So: right *shape*, about 8 km (5 mi) short on distance, and the closing
segment landed roughly 30–40 s/km above the LT band. Given it caps a 97 km target week,
banking a clean, controlled 18 km with a genuine negative split is a perfectly
reasonable trade — the aerobic stimulus was there and the HR stayed contained.
