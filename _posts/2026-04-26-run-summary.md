### Notes

A high-quality long run capping the week. The first eight kilometres rolled out controlled at ~5:30/km with HR in the 110s — patient warm-up done right. From km 9 the pace dropped under 5:15/km and held in the marathon-pace band through km 16, then dropped sharply to 4:26–4:44/km for five tempo kilometres before easing out. Stride length opened from 105 cm in the warm-up to 122 cm at the fastest split — pure aerobic strength, not strain. Average HR of 131 over nearly two hours and 22.58 km is an excellent aerobic signature. With proximal hamstring tendinopathy in the background, the long, gradual ramp was the right way to deliver tempo work — the tendon was thoroughly warmed before any speed went on. Caps a 82-ish km week in good form.
