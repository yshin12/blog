Warm and muggy. The dew point of 66 °F (18.9 °C) is above the 65 °F line where humidity starts to slow you down, and temperature plus dew point comes to 138 °F, just under the 140 °F threshold. That is worth about 2–3% on pace. The heat-adjusted endurance band is roughly **8:00–8:55 /mi (4:58–5:32 /km)**, compared with the nominal 7:50–8:41 /mi (4:52–5:24 /km). Some of the HR rise late in the run is heat drift, not just the faster pace.

### Notes

**Plan vs. actual:** the plan prescribed **endurance, 11 mi (18 km)**, and the run came in at 11.26 mi (18.12 km). The distance was on target. The structure was right too: Pfitz endurance runs are meant to get faster as they go, and this one did, getting quicker every mile from 8:47 to 7:22 /mi (5:28 to 4:35 /km) with no stalls.

**Intensity:** the first 7 miles stayed in the endurance band, which is ideal. Miles 8–11 at 7:29–7:36 /mi (4:39–4:43 /km) were about 15–20 s/mi faster than the fast end of the band. That puts them in the gap between endurance pace and goal HM pace of 6:49 /mi (4:14 /km). HR climbed to 146–151 bpm, about 83–86% of max, in a humid 72 °F. That's a solid aerobic workout, but it was a notch harder than an endurance run needs to be.

**Mechanics:** cadence held at 174–180 spm the whole way. Stride length grew from 1.02 m to 1.19–1.23 m, so the extra speed came from a longer stride, the same efficient pattern as Tuesday. Rolling terrain (161 m gain) makes the negative split more impressive.

**Hamstring/glute:** a strong, even progression with steady cadence and no late fade suggests no mechanical guarding. Still, the fast finish adds load right before a VO2max day. Do a few glute-bridge or isometric hamstring holds tonight, and check how the sit-bone area feels in the morning.

**Looking ahead:** tomorrow is the key session of peak week, **VO2max 11 mi (18 km) with 6 × 1,200 m at 6:02–6:18 /mi (3:45–3:55 /km)**. After today's quick last miles, keep the warm-up truly easy. If it's muggy again, aim for the slower end of the band and let effort guide you more than the watch. Bring water, and go easy for the first rep. If the hamstring is at 2/10 or worse in the morning, cut the session to 4–5 reps.
