### Why it counts as Recovery

- **HR stayed low the whole way:** it averaged 117 bpm (about 64% of max) and peaked at only 125 bpm, well under the ~138 bpm (76%) recovery ceiling.
- **Pace stayed in the band:** the 5:31 /km average sits inside the 5:25–5:55 /km recovery range. After km 2, every split fell between 5:24 and 5:30 /km.
- **No progression this time:** this was the fix suggested after yesterday's fast finish. The pace stayed level and HR held between 116 and 121 bpm from km 2 onward.
- **Form was smooth:** cadence settled at 178–181 spm and stride length held steady at about 1.00–1.02 m. Nothing in the splits suggests guarding the left hamstring/glute. The 4:42 /km "best pace" in km 1 looks like a brief GPS or start blip, not a surge.
