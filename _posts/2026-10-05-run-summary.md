### Plan adherence

- **Prescribed (race week, post-flu version):** a test run of 4 mi (6.4 km) easy at 8:45–9:30 /mi (5:25–5:55 /km), HR < 133, no strides.
- **Actual:** 4.06 mi with no strides, average HR 128, which is under the cap. Pace was about 20 s/mi quicker than the recovery band. That's still general-aerobic pace, and on a cool day HR is the better guide. HR went slightly over 133 only for a short stretch around mile 2.3 (135–137), then came back down to 132.
- **Go/no-go:** this was the key check, and it passed. Your recent easy runs at similar pace were 8:27 /mi @ 132 bpm (Sep 29), 8:48 /mi @ 125 (Sep 30) and 9:03 /mi @ 129 (Oct 3). Today was faster on equal or lower HR, so easy pace isn't costing more HR than usual.

### Notes

- **Good:** you held back with no strides. HR rose very gradually (115 → 132) and cadence stayed steady at 178–181 after the first km.
- **Watch:** keep tomorrow's warm-up just as relaxed. The pace crept faster over the last mile (8:14 /mi), so don't let the warm-up turn into a third goal-pace rep.
- **Hamstring/glute:** an easy, flat 4 miles carries very little risk. Keep doing the isometric bridge holds through Thursday.
