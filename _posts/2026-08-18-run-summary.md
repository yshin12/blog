**Run type: General Aerobic (AR) + speed.** Pfitzinger HM Schedule 2, weeks-to-goal 7, Tuesday —
prescribed "General aerobic, 9 miles (14 km)." Delivered 15.3 km (9.51 mi), so 1.3 km (0.8 mi) over,
with four ~100 m strides tacked on that the plan doesn't ask for until Saturday. Harmless
bonus at this HR.

### Shape of the run

The aerobic block covered 14 km in 1:13:20, averaging 5:14 /km (8:26 /mi) — right in the general
aerobic band of 5:00–5:40 /km (8:03–9:07 /mi). It opened conservatively at 5:29 /km (8:50 /mi) and
settled into 5:09–5:14 /km (8:17–8:26 /mi) from km 6 onward, with the quickest full kilometre at
5:09 /km (8:17 /mi). No spikes, no fade.

Heart rate is the real story: 116 bpm on the first kilometre drifting to 134–135 bpm by the end,
averaging 131 bpm. That sits mid-band for general aerobic (70–81% max, ~123–143 bpm) and shows
almost no cardiac drift across 75 minutes — the aerobic base is holding well after the recovery
week.

### Strides

Four strides of ~0.10–0.11 km at 3:39, 3:16, 3:30 and 3:28 /km (5:52, 5:15, 5:38 and 5:35 /mi),
each with a jog float of about 31 seconds, then a 0.59 km (0.37 mi) cooldown at 5:22 /km
(8:38 /mi). Cadence peaked at 209 spm and stride length stretched to 1.43 m on the first one —
turnover, not straining. Max HR only reached 151 bpm, confirming these were neuromuscular
efforts rather than anaerobic ones.

### Notes

Left hamstring/glute stayed quiet enough that the pace held steady and the strides were clean —
no split-to-split hesitation that would suggest guarding. Worth a check in the morning before
tomorrow's threshold session, which is the bigger ask: 16 km (9.94 mi) with a 22-minute LT
interval, 4 minutes jog, then an 18-minute LT interval at 4:03–4:09 /km (6:31–6:41 /mi). If the
hamstring reads 2/10 or higher on waking, trim the second interval rather than skipping the day.
