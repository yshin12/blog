## Plan check

Pfitzinger HM Schedule 2, 9 weeks to goal, prescribed a **progression long run of 23 km (14.3 mi)**
with the last 3 km (1.9 mi) at LT pace. Today came in at 6.45 km (4.01 mi) easy — a substantial
cutback. In context, though, the week still landed at roughly **90 km (56 mi) against an 84 km
(52 mi) target**, with the Wednesday LT session, Thursday endurance run and Saturday
speed workout all completed. The long run was the piece traded away, not the quality.

Cadence held at 171 spm with a 1.01 m stride — relaxed mechanics, no sign of compensation from the
left hamstring/glute, and the low, steady heart rate suggests the shakeout did its job.
