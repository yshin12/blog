**Conditions:** 20.0 °C (68 °F), feels like 20.0 °C (68 °F), dew point 12.8 °C
(55 °F), 64 % humidity, wind 17.7 km/h (11 mph) from the east, Burlington Piers.
Temp plus dew point came to 123 °F, well under the 140 °F line where humidity
starts costing time. This is the coolest, driest air you've run in all week — no
heat adjustment needed, and the easterly off the lake would have helped on the
exposed stretches.

**Structure.** The run split cleanly into three parts. First, a 9.5 km aerobic
body that drifted down from 5:36 /km (9:01 /mi) to 5:05–5:12 /km (8:11–8:22 /mi),
with HR climbing gently from 117 to 136 bpm. Then four strides: 17.9 s, 18.4 s,
21.2 s and 21.1 s, running 3:32, 3:44, 3:22 and 3:22 /km (5:41, 6:01, 5:25 and
5:25 /mi), each with a 50–90 s jog between. Then a 0.73 km cooldown at 5:49 /km
(9:22 /mi).

The first two strides were a touch conservative, outside the 3:20–3:37 /km
(5:22–5:49 /mi) speed band. The last two landed right on it at 3:22 /km, and
stride length stretched to 1.60 m on both. That is exactly the pattern you want
the night before a race — find the rhythm, then stop, rather than chasing times.

Pace-wise the aerobic portion ran faster than the 5:25–5:55 /km (8:45–9:30 /mi)
recovery band, but HR governs here, and 130 bpm average sits below the ~134 bpm
recovery ceiling. So the legs were moving well on low cardiac cost. Cadence at
174 spm is up from yesterday's 169, though still below your usual 178–181.

**Plan adherence.** Pfitzinger HM Schedule 2, 4 weeks to goal (Sep 7–13). Friday
calls for **Recovery 6 km (3.7 mi) + 4 × 100 m strides**. The strides were exactly
on script. The distance ran 5 km (3.1 mi) long, which is the one thing to flag —
the short Friday is deliberately short so the legs are fresh for a tune-up race
the next morning. HR was low enough that the damage is minimal, but keep it in
mind when you feel the first kilometre tomorrow.

Left hamstring and glute: nothing in the data suggests a problem. Stride length
stayed symmetrical through the fast reps and the pace progression was smooth, with
no split where you backed off. Check it on the first steps out of bed tomorrow.

**Week so far:** 58.61 km (36.4 mi) over four running days, against a plan week of
84 km (52.2 mi).

**Next:** **Saturday Sep 12 — 8K–10K tune-up race**, 16 km (10 mi) total with
warmup and cooldown. Target 4:00–4:05 /km (6:26–6:34 /mi), and start at the slow
end. Carb-focused breakfast about 3 hours out, electrolytes beforehand, and fluid
on the course if it is warm. If the hamstring reads 2/10 or worse in the morning,
run it as a controlled tempo instead of a race.
