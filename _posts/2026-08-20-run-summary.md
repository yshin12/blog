session, shifted a day to Thursday after a light 4 km shakeout yesterday. Two long threshold blocks
— 22 minutes and 18 minutes — split by a 4 minute jog, bookended by a proper warmup and cooldown.
The execution was almost exactly to the letter of the plan.

**Classification: LT (Lactate Threshold).** Total threshold volume 9.73 km (6.05 mi) in 40:34 at an
average of 4:10 /km (6:42 /mi) — right at the top edge of the 4:03–4:09 /km (6:31–6:41 /mi) LT band,
which is exactly where a 40-minute total threshold load should sit.

### How it went

The pacing discipline stands out. Block 1 ran 4:10, 4:10, 4:10, 4:07, 4:07 — barely three seconds
of drift across 22 minutes. Block 2 came back at 4:08, 4:12, 4:12, 4:10 after only four minutes of
recovery, which is the harder ask, and it held within four seconds of block 1. That is a genuinely
well-judged threshold effort rather than a tempo that starts hot and fades.

Heart rate tells the same story: block 1 climbed from 131 to a steady 147–155, block 2 settled into
145–153, and it never ran away. Cadence during the fast work sat at 191–198 spm with stride length
stretching to 125 cm, up from 113 cm in the warmup — the speed came from turnover plus stride, not
from straining. The cooldown dropped back to 5:40 /km with HR under 130, a clean shutdown.

### Plan adherence

Prescribed for the week-7 Wednesday slot (Aug 17–23, 89 km week): *Lactate threshold — 16 km; 22 min
LT interval, jog 4 min recovery, 18 min LT interval.* Actual: 15.01 km with 22:24 and 18:10 blocks
and a 4:01 jog between them. The interval durations and the recovery are essentially perfect; total
distance came up 1 km short, which is immaterial. The day swap with Wednesday's short jog is a
neutral, sensible adaptation — the session got done fresh rather than tired.

### Notes

The left hamstring/glute area got its hardest test since the niggle appeared, and the data shows no
sign of trouble: no split-to-split pace decay, no cadence drop-off, stride length symmetrical with
prior sessions. Worth checking how it feels tomorrow morning — if anything reads at 2/10 or above,
Sunday's progression long run with 5 km at LT is the piece to soften first.
