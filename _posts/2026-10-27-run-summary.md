### Hamstring / glute

This was the heaviest load on the left hamstring since the race: four fast kilometres plus sprint-speed strides. The splits show no guarding. Stride length stayed long through rep 4 and the cool-down didn't slow. Proximal hamstring tendons tend to react the next morning, though, so check with a firm-chair sit and a single-leg bridge on waking. At **2/10 or more**, keep the week easy and drop the strides. Keep up the isometric bridge holds; skip deep stretching.
