### Notes

A textbook progression: a long, patient warm-up over the first six kilometres (8:18 → 6:04/km, HR 98–116) eased into a steadily accelerating second half that finished at 4:09/km — within a hair of 5K race pace. HR climbed almost monotonically with pace and topped out at 160 in the closing kilometre, but average HR stayed at 121 thanks to the long aerobic opener; time-in-zone was Z2-dominant (~37 minutes) with only ~12 minutes in Z4–Z5. Cadence stabilised at 175 spm from km 2 onward and ticked up to 185 spm during the hot finish, with stride length opening from 80 cm in the warm-up to 129 cm at the close — the classic signature of more turnover *and* more reach as effort lifts. Fastest splits: 1 km in 4:09.9, 1 mi in 6:45.6, 5 km in 23:28.7, 10 km in 56:55. With proximal hamstring tendinopathy in the picture, the very gradual ramp was protective — load was added only after tissue was thoroughly warm — but the final two kilometres at 4:20/4:09 were genuinely brisk and constitute the hardest running of the week.
