### Classification rationale

An hour of running at 5:42/km with average HR of 124 and a maximum of 137 is an
**easy aerobic run** by any measure. The pace drifted down gently (5:54 → 5:28)
across the middle of the run, but the last full kilometre came back to 5:59 and
the session finished on a jog — so this reads as natural rhythm settling in, not
a structured progression or a workout finish. HR never left the aerobic band.
Distance and duration place it as a standard daily easy run rather than an MLR.
