The opening kilometre at 6:55 /km (11:07 /mi) was a genuine walk-it-in warmup — HR 111, stride
only 0.90 m — and from there the run settled and drifted gently faster. The first half averaged
5:58 /km (9:36 /mi) and the second half 5:30 /km (8:51 /mi), a 28 s/km negative split achieved
without heart rate ever leaving the easy zone: 123 bpm average, peaking at 136 bpm (~79% max) on
the fastest kilometre. Cadence held 170 spm and stride length grew 0.90 m → 1.07 m across the run,
so the speed came from opening up rather than forcing turnover on tired legs.

### Context

This is the second day of the recovery week (weeks-to-goal 8, 75 km target), and the week has no
hard workouts on the schedule — general aerobic running plus strides Thursday and Saturday, then
an 18 km endurance run Sunday. After a 90 km week capped by Monday's 23 km progression, banking an
easy hour here protects the back half of the week, where Wednesday's 16 km (10 mi) general aerobic
run is the larger ask.

The left hamstring/glute area came through yesterday's fast finish and today's easy hour without
anything showing up in the data — no pace drift, no cadence drop-off, no asymmetric slowdown late.
Two consecutive days of clean running on that region is the encouraging trend heading into the
October 11 build.
