### Notes

A textbook MLR to wrap the first full week back from the Toronto HM PB (1:28:39). The pace pattern is exactly what you want to see for an aerobic builder: a relaxed 6:36 opener, a 6 km mid-block in the 5:50s, and a clean negative-split second half drifting down to 5:27/km — all while HR never crossed 132 (max) and averaged a very tidy 118 bpm. Cadence sat at 168–172 spm throughout, stride length grew from 0.99 m to 1.08 m as legs warmed up, and the effort was clearly aerobic the entire way.

PHT was a non-issue today — no shortened stride, no HR drift signaling compensation, and the willingness to extend pace late in the run is a good sign that the proximal tendon tolerated 13 km of steady loading well. Weekly total now closes at **~63 km** across 8 sessions, the highest post-PB week so far. **Next:** an easy/recovery day or short shakeout to open the new week — keep the long-run rhythm without rushing the tendon.
