## Activity 1 — VO2max intervals (peak week key session)

Thursday of peak week, three weeks out from the 2026-10-11 half. The plan called for 6 × 1,200 m, with a pre-agreed abort rule set that morning: **if rep 4 is slower than 4:45, stop at five reps.** Rep 4 came in at 4:45.8. The session stopped at five. That was the plan working, not the runner quitting.

Mild but sticky for an evening interval session. Temperature plus dew point comes to 131 °F, well under the 140 °F line, so humidity alone was not the story — but 70 °F is still 10 °F above ideal interval weather, worth roughly 1–2 % on pace. The heat-adjusted VO2max band is about **6:06–6:26 /mi (3:47–4:00 /km)**, which puts reps 1–3 comfortably inside it and reps 4–5 right at the edge rather than off the map.

### Notes

**What the HR says.** This is the useful signal. HR climbed on every rep — 148, 153, 157, 160, 162 — with max touching 169 on rep 4. Effort was rising while pace was falling, which is the signature of peripheral fatigue, dehydration and heat, not central fatigue or a fitness drop. If under-fuelling had capped the system, HR would have flattened or fallen on the slow reps. It did the opposite. The engine was working; the fuel and fluid were not there to convert it.

**Two things to fix, both off the road.** No fluid was carried for roughly 90 minutes of evening running — at a 1–1.5 L/h sweat rate that is a 2 %-plus body-mass loss by rep 4, exactly where the times went. On top of that, two consecutive days of a roughly 1,240 kcal energy deficit and Wednesday's carbs at about 3.7 g/kg against a 6–7 g/kg target. And Wednesday's endurance run finished at 7:29–7:36 /mi (4:39–4:43 /km) with HR to 151, faster than the endurance band, the day before intervals. Three small debts came due on one session.

**Mechanics were clean.** Cadence held at 202–209 spm across all five reps with no decay. Stride length shortened from 1.25 m to 1.21 m, a 3 % drop over 6,000 m of hard running, which is ordinary fatigue. Had the hamstring been guarding, cadence would have risen while stride collapsed, or the drop would have been abrupt. It was gradual and symmetric with the pace curve. The five strides beforehand at 19–20 s per 100 m were sharp and relaxed.

**Plan vs. actual.** Prescribed: 11 mi (18 km) with 6 × 1,200 m. Actual: 10.07 mi (16.21 km) with 5 × 1,200 m. The missing mile is the missing rep plus its recovery. Stopping on a pre-set rule three weeks from a goal race is the right call every time — pushing a sixth rep at 4:50-plus would have bought fatigue and no adaptation.

**Hamstring/glute.** No warning signs in the data. Isometric holds tonight rather than stretching, and check the sit-bone in the morning.

**Looking ahead.** Friday is recovery, 6 mi (10 km), genuinely easy — do not add the missing rep back. Saturday is general aerobic 10 mi (16 km), and Sunday's 16 mi (26 km) long run is the session that actually matters this week. Carry water and take carbs seriously between now and then.
