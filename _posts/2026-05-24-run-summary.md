## PHT Watch (14 days post-flare)

A controlled 5:15/km pace with 173 spm cadence and no long-stride mechanics is **gentle on the proximal hamstring**. The final-km surge with 196 spm and 1.21 m stride was short enough not to be a risk, and the cadence-led acceleration is the right way to pick up pace (quick turnover rather than long reach). No signs of compensation in the data — pacing stayed even, HR built linearly, splits got faster not slower late in the run.

Two weeks out from the 2026-05-10 flare and the trend is encouraging: easy aerobic work at this volume is the perfect rehab load. Keep skipping deep hamstring stretches; isometric holds (long-lever bridge, single-leg deadlift hold) remain the anchor.

A quietly excellent maintenance day — low cost, good rhythm, and a confidence-building close. 👟
