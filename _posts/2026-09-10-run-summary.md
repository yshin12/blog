Two easy runs today, two days before Saturday's 8K–10K tune-up race. Together they
came to **17.05 km (10.59 mi)**, and HR never went above 134 bpm.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|

| Distance | 7.05 km (4.38 mi) |
| Time | 44:54 |
| Avg pace | 6:22 /km (10:15 /mi) |
| Best pace | 5:47 /km (9:18 /mi) |
| Avg HR | 114 bpm |
| Max HR | 123 bpm |
| Avg cadence | 179 spm |
| Avg stride length | 0.87 m |

**Conditions:** 25.0 °C (77 °F), dew point 12.2 °C (54 °F), 44 % humidity, wind
16.1 km/h (10 mph) WNW, Burlington Piers. Warm but dry. Temperature plus dew point
came to 131 °F, which is below the range where humidity starts to cost time.

This was a very easy shuffle. Every split fell between 6:09 and 6:42 /km
(9:54–10:47 /mi), slower than the recovery band's 5:25 /km (8:45 /mi) ceiling,
and HR stayed between 107 and 118 bpm. Cadence held about 179 spm even at this
pace, with a short 0.85–0.90 m stride. That's a gentle way to load a hamstring
you're keeping an eye on.

| Avg cadence | 169 spm |
| Avg stride length | 1.04 m |
| Elevation gain / loss | 30 m / 28 m |
| Calories | 705 kcal |

**Conditions:** 22.2 °C (72 °F), dew point 11.1 °C (52 °F), 50 % humidity, wind
14.5 km/h (9 mph) WNW. It was cooler and just as dry, with a temp+dew sum of 124 °F.

This run sped up smoothly, from 6:07–6:13 /km (9:50–10:00 /mi) at the start to
5:24 /km (8:41 /mi) for the last two kilometres. HR rose steadily from 110 to
128 bpm as the pace picked up, so the extra speed didn't come with any drift. The
whole run stayed under the ~134 bpm recovery ceiling (76 % of max). The last 4 km
were at the fast edge of the recovery band, 5:25–5:55 /km (8:45–9:30 /mi), which
is fine this time but a little quick two days before a race.

One thing to watch: cadence averaged 169 spm and stride length 1.04 m. That's well
off your usual 178–181 spm. A longer, lower-cadence stride means reaching forward,
which loads the proximal hamstring more, so aim for 175+ spm on easy runs.

**Plan adherence:** Pfitzinger HM Schedule 2, 4 weeks to goal (Sep 7–13). Thursday
calls for **Recovery, 11 km (7 mi)**. The intensity matched the plan exactly, but
splitting the day into two runs added about 6 km (3.7 mi). HR was this low, so
the cost is small. Splitting the distance also kept each run short and let the legs
recover in between. The week now projects to about 88.6 km (55.1 mi) against the
planned 84 km (52.2 mi).

**Next:** Friday is **Recovery, 6 km (3.7 mi) + 4 × 100 m strides** at
5:30–5:55 /km (8:51–9:30 /mi). Keep it exactly that length. On **Saturday Sep 12**
comes the **8K–10K tune-up race** at 4:00–4:05 /km (6:26–6:34 /mi). Start at the
slow end of that range. Eat a proper carb-focused breakfast and drink electrolytes
beforehand. If the left hamstring or glute is at 2/10 or worse on Friday morning,
skip the strides and run Saturday as a controlled effort.
