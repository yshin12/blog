### Notes

A clean, well-shaped progression run nine days after the Toronto HM PB. Opened at a deliberate 6:54/km with HR in the 110s, settled into the 6:20s through km 4 with HR actually dropping into the 100s, then methodically lifted the pace by ~25 sec/km every couple of kilometres until closing with two solid 4:31/km efforts. The HR response was textbook — only 140 bpm at LT pace, suggesting strong aerobic fitness retention from the marathon block. Cadence climbed appropriately with effort (164 → 179 spm) and stride length stretched from 0.87 m at the warm-up to 1.22 m at the LT close.

PHT behaved well through the gear-changes: stride length opened cleanly at faster paces (a good sign that the proximal tendon is loading without inhibition), cadence stayed high enough to keep the foot under the hip, and there were no signs of asymmetry or HR drift at constant pace. Finishing with two reps rather than three was a smart conservative call — first real tempo touch since the HM, and the tendon got a meaningful but not aggressive stimulus. **Next:** an easy 8–10 km recovery jog tomorrow at ~5:40–6:00/km, then keep the rest of the week aerobic before considering another quality touch on the weekend.
