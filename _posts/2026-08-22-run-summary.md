Two activities today, taken back to back on a Saturday morning: a genuinely easy 2.42 km opener at
8:31, a fifteen minute pause, then a 5.04 km progression that stepped down every kilometre and
finished with a short burst. A light day in volume terms — well under the prescribed 13 km — but the
second piece was sharp, controlled and well executed.

The sequencing was sound: a slow 15 minute opener at HR 124, a short standing break, then straight
into the quality piece. That is a warmup-plus-effort structure logged as two files rather than a
genuine double, and the HR data confirms it — run 2 started at 142 bpm rather than from cold, so the
opener did its job. Total cardiac load was modest, with only the last two kilometres above 150 bpm.

**Plan adherence.** Week 7 of the Pfitzinger build (Aug 17–23, 89 km target) prescribes for Saturday:
*general aerobic + speed, 13 km (8 mi), 2 sets of 6 × 100 m strides.* Actual was 7.46 km (4.64 mi)
with a single short surge instead of twelve strides. The intensity intent was partly met — the
progression touched faster-than-aerobic running and the closing burst hit stride pace — but the
volume came up about 5.5 km short and the strides were not done as prescribed. With Friday's recovery
run also skipped, the week sits at 41.8 km (26.0 mi) through Saturday against an 89 km (55 mi) target.
Tomorrow's progression long run — 23 km (14.3 mi) with the last 5 km (3.1 mi) at LT pace of
4:03–4:09 /km (6:31–6:41 /mi) — is the session that matters most this week, and today's light load
leaves plenty in the legs for it.

**Left hamstring/glute.** Today was a low-risk day for the niggle: short, and the faster running came
progressively rather than from a standing start. No pace decay, no cadence drop, stride length
consistent with recent sessions. Two days on from Thursday's threshold work that is a reassuring
read. Worth a check before tomorrow's long run — if anything reads 2/10 or above, shortening the
closing LT segment from 5 km to 3 km is the first adjustment to make, ahead of cutting overall
distance.
