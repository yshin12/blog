### Why it counts as General Aerobic + speed

- **The steady portion was relaxed aerobic running:** it averaged 5:19 /km, inside the 5:00–5:40 /km AR band. HR stayed at 117–130 bpm (about 64–71% of max), which is at or just below the 123–143 bpm AR range. Running at the low end of AR is exactly right in a recovery week.
- **There was a natural progression with almost no HR cost:** pace went from 5:36 to 5:01 /km while HR rose only from about 118 to 128 bpm. Cadence climbed from 171 to 188 spm over the same stretch. That points to legs that are coming back well from the race.
- **The strides were very consistent:** all eight took 19.7–20.3 s and ran at 3:06–3:12 /km, with stride length opening to about 1.42–1.53 m. That pace is a touch quicker than the 3:20–3:37 /km speed band, so keep them at "fast but relaxed" rather than sprinting. HR peaked at only 155 bpm and recovered between reps.
- **Nothing in the data suggests guarding:** there was no fade and no uneven reps, and cadence held smooth throughout. This was a good first test for both the recently resolved left groin and the left hamstring/glute niggle. If either feels tender tomorrow morning, skip strides for the next few days.
