That is not a problem for fitness, but this is a taper week with a tune-up in two days. Keep Friday short: the planned **4 mi (6 km) recovery + 4 × 100 m strides** at 8:45–9:30 /mi (5:25–5:55 /km), with strides of 20–22 s at about 5:22–5:49 /mi (3:20–3:37 /km).

Do the sit test and a single-leg bridge on Friday morning before deciding on tune-up intensity. If either reads 2/10 or higher, run Saturday as a controlled threshold effort.
