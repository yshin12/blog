One run today: 17.72 km in Burlington with about 300 m of climbing, the day after a 19:49 parkrun. It was planned as an easy, flat 12–14 km, so it ended up longer and harder than intended.

The first four km were properly easy at HR 118–124. The second climb went 16 s/km faster than the first, and cadence stayed near 185 spm on most of both climbs. On the descent, km 11–12 came in at 4:20 and 4:14 /km with cadence up at 188 spm.

The 142 average HR makes this look easier than it was. About 47 of the 88 minutes were above my 143 easy ceiling, so this wasn't an easy day. The three pickups at the end were also faster than they needed to be, so I'll leave those out for now.

Today's climbing was also a real load for the left hamstring/glute. Cadence and stride show no sign of guarding, but I'll check for sit-bone soreness tomorrow morning.
