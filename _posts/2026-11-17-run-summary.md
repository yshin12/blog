### Notes

- **Very even pacing.** All 15 of the 400 m laps in the reps fell between 3:58 and 4:06 /km, and rep 3 matched rep 1.
- **A touch quick for threshold.** The LT band is 4:03–4:09 /km, and all three reps sat at its fast edge. Heart rate settled at 170–172, which is closer to a 10K effort. Next time, start rep 1 at about 4:10 and take about 2 min of jogging between 2 km reps.
