### Why the rest matters

Seven straight days and 113 km is a genuinely heavy block, and this coming week
carries the **8K–10K tune-up race on Saturday Sep 12** — the first real sharpening
test before the 2026-10-11 half marathon. Absorbing last week's volume with a full
day off is the right sequencing: it protects the quality of Tuesday's strides and
leaves the legs fresh enough that Saturday's tune-up can actually measure fitness
rather than fatigue.

Rest is also the cheapest possible insurance for the **left hamstring/glute niggle**.
A zero-load day gives the proximal tendon a full recovery cycle with no eccentric
stress at all. If any residual soreness is present, keep to gentle isometric
holds — long-lever bridges or an isometric hamstring hold, 5 × 30–45 s — and avoid
aggressive static hamstring stretching, which compresses the tendon at its
insertion. If the area feels clean tomorrow morning, Tuesday's strides are fine
to run as written.
