**Run type: Easy / Aerobic base.**

### This is exactly the right session

Yesterday was four hard reps with HR to 169 — against the standing post-collapse instruction. Today is the correction, and it's a clean one. Average HR of **128 bpm** with a max of only **146** is genuinely easy: no drift into tempo or threshold territory, no progression finish, no surge at the end. Pace sat at 5:46 /km, comfortably slower than your usual 5:00–5:30 easy range, which means you ran by effort rather than by the watch. That's the correct instinct in July heat.

Distance is not the issue — 11.2 km is fine and well within what you've been doing since the cap was lifted. Intensity was the issue, and today it was managed properly.

### What the numbers say

- **Cadence held at 180 spm** with a 0.98 m stride — relaxed turnover, no shuffling despite the easy pace. Form didn't degrade over 65 minutes.
- **HR ceiling of 146** on a warm day at 11 km is a good aerobic signal. The gap between avg (128) and max (146) is narrow, meaning the run stayed genuinely flat in effort.
- **811 kcal** over the hour — worth replacing deliberately. Post-collapse, under-fueling is the risk to avoid, not the goal to chase.

### Still outstanding

The **stress echocardiogram** result from early July still needs confirming, and the **GI and Cardiology follow-ups** remain pending. Those appointments — not the calendar, and not how good you feel — are what should gate any return to intervals, tempo, or racing. Until then, days like today are the whole plan.

Hydration and fueling stay front of mind in mid-July. Easy runs like this one, done consistently, rebuild the base without asking anything of a system that was under real strain six weeks ago.

Good session. More of these.
