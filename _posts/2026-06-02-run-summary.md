A classic split-session day: an easy warm-up, a sharp 5×1km interval block, and a short cool-down jog. Three activities, one solid quality day.

## Activity 1 — Warm-up (Easy)

- **Type:** Easy / Warm-up
- **Distance:** 4.07 km
- **Time:** 20:48
- **Avg pace:** 5:06 /km (best 4:34 in the strides)
- **Avg / Max HR:** 133 / 158 bpm
- **Cadence:** 169 spm · **Stride:** 1.13 m
- **Calories:** 298

Smooth progression from 5:16 to 4:51, finishing with a few faster strides to prime the legs — exactly how a warm-up should build.

## Activity 2 — Interval Workout (5×1km)

- **Type:** Interval (VO2 / LT)
- **Distance:** 6.82 km
- **Time:** 29:12
- **Avg pace:** 4:16 /km (work reps 3:50–3:56 /km, best 3:36)
- **Avg / Max HR:** 161 / 171 bpm
- **Cadence:** 190 spm · **Stride:** 1.20 m
- **Calories:** 509

Five 1 km reps with ~2 min float recoveries. Rep splits were metronomic — **3:50, 3:52, 3:52, 3:55, 3:56** — barely a few seconds of drift across the set despite HR climbing rep to rep (152 → 167 bpm). Cadence held near 199 spm on the hard efforts. Strong, controlled execution.

## Activity 3 — Cool-down (Easy)

- **Type:** Easy / Cool-down
- **Distance:** 1.38 km
- **Time:** 08:26
- **Avg pace:** 6:06 /km
- **Avg / Max HR:** 131 / 135 bpm
- **Cadence:** 165 spm · **Stride:** 1.00 m
- **Calories:** 110

Easy shake-out to flush the legs and bring HR down. Good discipline keeping it genuinely slow.

## Day as a Whole

- **Combined distance:** 12.27 km
- **Combined time:** ~58:25
- **Total calories:** 917
- **HR load:** peaked at 171 bpm in the intervals; bookended by easy aerobic work in the 131–133 bpm range
- **Sequencing:** Textbook quality-day structure — warm-up (4.07 km) → intervals (6.82 km) → cool-down (1.38 km). Effort was concentrated cleanly in the middle block, with proper on/off ramps either side.

A well-built interval day with tight rep consistency and sensible bookends.

### PHT note

Intervals raise faster-pace loading on the proximal hamstring, but rep splits stayed even with no late-rep collapse — a good sign there was no compensation or guarding. Keep cool-down easy, skip aggressive hamstring stretching, and hold the isometric routine (long-hold bridges / iso wall holds) as the rehab anchor.
