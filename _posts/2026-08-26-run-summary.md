One activity today: a short, deliberately gentle 4.5 km shakeout. Average pace 6:51 /km with an
average heart rate of just 112 bpm — well below even the recovery band, which makes this an
easy-day / niggle-management jog rather than a training stimulus. Nothing wrong with a day like
this in the middle of a 92 km plan week.

5:25–5:55 /km recovery ceiling and heart rate never cracked 126 bpm, so this was aerobic
maintenance and blood flow, nothing more.

### Plan note

The Pfitzinger week (6 weeks to the 2026-10-11 half) had a VO2max session pencilled in for today
— 3×1200 m plus 3×1000 m. That was traded for this easy jog instead. Given the left
hamstring/glute niggle still on the watch list, swapping hard intervals for a low-load shakeout is
a sensible call; the short stride and low heart rate suggest the leg was being protected rather
than pushed. The VO2max work can move later in the week if the leg stays quiet.
