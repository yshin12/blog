### Classification

This was an **Interval (VO2max)** session. 3:51 /km is in the middle of the VO2max band (3:45–3:55 /km, 3K–5K race pace). At that fixed speed, peak HR rose 11 beats across the six reps and the last two reached max (177–178). That is normal drift at a constant pace, and the pace didn't fade. The plan had called for a lactate-threshold session at 4:03–4:09 /km with HR at or under 160, so the session that was run was one intensity level higher than planned.

- **To improve:** at this pace, jog 2:00 between reps. From rep 3 onwards HR only came back down to the 140s. Eat more before a session like this: only about 1,135 kcal had been logged before the 17:00 start.
