### Notes

Prescribed for Tuesday of the peak week (102 km) was **general aerobic, 14 km (9 mi)** — delivered almost to the metre at 14.56 km (9.05 mi). The shape was ideal: miles 1–2 as a true warm-up shuffle, then a smooth progression from 5:37 /km down to 5:01 /km (9:02 → 8:05 /mi), finishing in the endurance band. Average HR of 125 bpm sits right in the 70–81 % general aerobic window, and the last four miles at 5:00–5:08 /km cost only 134–138 bpm — strong aerobic efficiency heading into the biggest week of the build. Cadence held 177 spm with stride length growing from 0.83 m to 1.10 m, i.e. the speed came from opening the stride, not from churning.

Easy day banked with no red flags. Wednesday's 18 km endurance run and Thursday's 6 × 1,200 m VO2max session are the week's real work.
