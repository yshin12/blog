## Day as a Whole

Sequencing was textbook: 3.84 km warmup → 4.87 km interval block → 1.82 km cooldown, total 10.53 km in just under 52 minutes. The cardiac story is well-managed: the warmup brought HR into the 130s, the reps stepped it into the high-140s/low-150s (peak 165 on the final rep), and the cooldown returned HR comfortably to the low 140s. Calorie load of 761 kcal is moderate — this was a quality session, not a volume bomb. As a return-to-speed effort post-PHT, the structure (true warmup + measured rep count + jog recoveries + cooldown) was exactly right.

## PHT Watch (16 days post-flare)

This is the first real speed work since the May 10 flare, and the data is genuinely encouraging. Cadence stayed in the protective 180+ spm range even at 3:53/km pace, stride length climbed sensibly (no over-striding), and the final rep was wisely backed off rather than forced. Heart rate response was clean — no HR drift mid-rep that would suggest a compensatory stride.

That said, intervals at 5K pace are among the higher-risk sessions for the proximal hamstring tendon. Tomorrow should be **easy or full rest**, and any glute/hamstring soreness in the next 24-48 h is the key signal. Keep the isometric work going (long-lever bridges, single-leg deadlift holds, 30-45s × 3-4 reps) — still the rehab anchor. Continue to avoid deep hamstring stretches.

A meaningful milestone session on the birthday: the engine still has top-end gears, the legs handled real speed, and the tendon was respected. 🎂👟
