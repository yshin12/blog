The 400 m alternation pattern is also gentler on the tendon than continuous tempo because the float recoveries unload the proximal hamstring every other lap. Cadence on the curves drifted lower than usual (165 spm) — something to tighten up next time.

### Day as a Whole

A smart double: aerobic/turnover work in the afternoon, sharper but short tempo work in the evening, with a ~3.5 h gap to refuel between them. The afternoon track session primed leg turnover without depleting the legs, and the evening tempo confirmed that LT fitness is well-preserved post-HM. Combined 12.24 km / 1:08:48 / 878 kcal is a moderate total — quality over volume.

PHT held up well across both sessions. Both favored higher cadence and shorter ground-contact time over slow compressive loading, which is exactly what the proximal hamstring needs three days post-flare. No glute signals during or after either run. **Next:** easy 8–10 km recovery jog tomorrow at ~5:40/km to let the tendon settle, then keep Friday aerobic and consider an MLR on the weekend.
