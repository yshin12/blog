### Notes

A clean, textbook negative-split tempo effort — five kilometres descending from 4:46 to 4:01, with HR climbing in lock-step from 136 to 163 bpm. PHT held up well through the gear-changes. No flare signals from the left glute. Second quality session of the week after Tuesday's progression run, and the body is responding without complaint. **Next:** easy 8–10 km recovery jog tomorrow at ~5:40/km to let the tendon settle, then keep Friday aerobic and consider an MLR on the weekend.
