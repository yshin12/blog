## Late-Night Treadmill Run — 8.0 km

A single treadmill session, started at 21:12 — mostly easy, but with one segment that ran too hot.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 8.00 km |
| Time | 42:09 |
| Avg Pace | 5:16 /km |
| Best Pace | 4:17 /km |
| Avg HR | 127 bpm |
| Max HR | 160 bpm |
| Cadence | 186 spm |
| Stride Length | 0.97 m |
| Calories | 530 kcal |

**Run type: Easy / Aerobic — with a surge that crossed the line.**

### Mostly right, one part wrong

The average tells a good story: **127 bpm** is genuinely easy, right where the last two weeks of running have lived. But the best pace of **4:17 /km** and a max HR of **160** say there was a hard segment in this run — threshold-effort territory, not easy running. That segment goes against the standing post-collapse instruction: everything easy until Cardiology clears harder work. The stress echo result and the GI/Cardiology follow-ups are still the gate, and they haven't opened yet.

Distance is not the concern — 8 km is fine. The surge is. Two days ago the same pattern appeared and was corrected the next day; the correction needs to stick.

### What the numbers say

- **Cadence at 186 spm** (max 202) with a 0.97 m stride — turnover stayed quick and light throughout.
- The **33-bpm gap between avg and max HR** is the tell: a flat easy run shows a narrow gap (Wednesday's was 18). Today's width means the effort spiked, not drifted.
- **530 kcal** burned late in the evening — refuel deliberately before bed rather than sleeping on empty; avoiding hypoglycemia is still a named priority after June 6.

### The late-night factor

Finishing a run within an hour of bedtime raises core temperature and heart rate right when the body should be winding down, which can shave sleep quality on exactly the night recovery matters most. When the schedule forces an evening run, keeping it fully easy — no surges — softens that cost. Tonight had both the late finish and the surge; the combination is worth avoiding.

Keep the runs easy — all of them, all the way through. The fitness will keep.
