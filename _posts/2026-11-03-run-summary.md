The jog added easy volume at very low effort, and it came well before the main run. Almost all of the day's load came from the last 30 minutes of the second run.

The plan was an easy 10–12 km on a flat route at 5:00–5:40 /km, with HR 123–143. The route was flat, but the distance ran over and only the first two km of the main run stayed in the pace band. With Saturday's parkrun and Sunday's hilly 17.72 km, that makes three demanding runs in four days, so the next few days need to stay truly easy. Tomorrow is a flat 8 km recovery run at 5:25–5:55 /km with HR under 134.

The faster running also loaded my left hamstring/glute more than planned. Cadence and stride show no sign of guarding, but I still need to check it tomorrow morning with a firm chair, a single-leg bridge and stairs.
