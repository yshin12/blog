The 10 km block averaged about 5:22 /km with HR at 119–131 bpm, which is low in the general aerobic band (123–143 bpm). HR crept up only about 10 bpm across the run, so there was little drift. Cadence settled at 176 spm.

The strides were on target: all five took 21–22 s per 100 m, at 3:26–3:30 /km, inside the speed band (3:20–3:37 /km). They were also very consistent, and stride length grew from 1.35 to 1.42 m, so they look relaxed rather than forced. HR during the strides peaked in the 150s. It then reached 160–166 bpm during the final short jog, which is a normal lag after back-to-back strides and nothing to worry about. Calories were about 783 kcal for the hour.
