Then you settled right on goal pace and averaged **6:46 /mi (4:12 /km) through mile 11**, which is on track for about 1:29. **Fuel:** you took gels before the start and at miles 4, 7 and 10.5, plus drinks at every station. Fuel was clearly enough, because energy didn't run out. The mild bloating suggests you can cut back on fluid at a 63°F (17°C) race. No FatSecret entries were logged today. Recovery meals should give about 1.0–1.2 g/kg of carbs plus 25–30 g of protein.

**Body check:** the left adductor/groin, first felt after Tuesday's strides, is sorer after the race. Walk only for the next few days. Don't run until walking is pain-free and the pillow-squeeze test is at most 1–2/10. See a doctor if you feel pain when you cough, notice a bulge, the pain gets worse each day, or you start to limp. The left hamstring/glute gave no signs of trouble in the data: cadence and stride were even until the general fade.
