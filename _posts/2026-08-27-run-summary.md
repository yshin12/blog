06:18, then a short strides set at 06:47. Total 6.29 km (3.91 mi) of running with heart rate
never leaving the aerobic range. A light day in volume terms, but the second piece added
genuine neuromuscular quality.

**Sequencing.** The order was correct: a 26-minute aerobic run to raise core temperature and
open the hips, a short break, then five relaxed strides on fully warm legs. That is the safest
way to introduce speed, and the progressive pace within both pieces shows the body responded
well rather than being forced.

**Load.** Low. Total time under 35 minutes, average HR in the 120s, and only about two minutes
of actual fast running. Calorie burn of 432 kcal reflects a light morning.

### Plan note

The Pfitzinger week 6 schedule called for a 19 km (11.8 mi) endurance run today, so 6.29 km is
well short of the prescribed volume. With the left hamstring/glute niggle still on the watch
list and Wednesday's VO2max session already traded for an easy jog, a short aerobic run plus
controlled strides is a reasonable way to keep some speed in the legs while keeping tissue load
low. The strides were fast but brief and on warm legs — low risk for the proximal hamstring.
If the leg stays quiet, the endurance mileage is the piece to rebuild first, with Sunday's
24 km (14.9 mi) endurance run the anchor of the week.
