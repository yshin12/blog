The load was concentrated in one 20-minute block, set up by a warm-up with pickups. The Hamilton jogs started about 1.5 and 3 hours after the parkrun, so they were separate short outings rather than a cool-down, and they added only 2.42 km.

The plan was 11 km of general aerobic running at 5:00–5:40 /km, so the day came in 1.42 km short and much harder. That was a fair swap for a fitness check. The fast running was the main test for the left hamstring/glute, and the steady stride and rising cadence show no sign of guarding. There's no pain report yet, so I'll check for sit-bone soreness tomorrow morning.
