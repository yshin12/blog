## PHT Watch (20 days post-flare)

A genuine red-zone day for the proximal hamstring — fast running loads the tendon more than easy mileage. Mitigating factors today: high cadence (192.8 spm) kept ground-contact short, stride at 1.32 m stayed under the over-stride threshold, terrain was flat (20 m gain / 19 m loss), and the hard volume was capped at 5 km. No surge to true max-out — HR peaked at 173, below all-out. This is exactly the kind of *short, sharp, controlled* threshold session the tendon can usually absorb.

Tonight and tomorrow morning: **5×45 s long-lever bridge isometrics** are non-negotiable after a fast effort like this. Watch for any deep-glute pull or sit-bone tenderness in the next 24–48 h — if it shows up, back off. Keep avoiding aggressive seated hamstring stretches, foam-roll the calves and adductors instead, and treat tomorrow as an easy 8–10 km recovery shake-out (or off entirely if anything feels stiff).

Strong, smart, sub-PB-adjacent. A confident birthday gear-check on a quiet tendon. 🎂👟
