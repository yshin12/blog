### Classification rationale

Ten flat-ish kilometres at 5:39/km with an average HR of 125 bpm and a max of only
140 bpm — this is a textbook easy aerobic run. HR never left the low aerobic band,
the pace band stayed inside a ~50 s/km window driven mostly by terrain, and there

### Notes

- **Correctly easy — a good correction after yesterday.** Yesterday's session was a
  5 × 1 km interval workout flagged as against current medical advice. Today's run
  is exactly the opposite profile: HR capped at 140, effort conversational
  throughout. This is the kind of run that should make up the training right now.
- **Clean HR control.** Average 125 bpm for 10 km is low aerobic. Even the fastest
  kilometre (5:23, last one) only reached 135 avg / 140 max, meaning the pace came
  from feeling loose rather than from pushing.
- **Sensible warmup.** First km at 6:02 with HR 108 — a genuine easing-in rather than

- **Terrain handled well.** Kms 6 and 9 carried the climbs (+5 m, +9 m); the pace
  gave way rather than the heart rate spiking, which is the right instinct on an
  easy day.
- **Cadence lower than usual.** 169 spm avg vs the typical 180+. That is partly just
  the slower easy pace, but stride length of 1.04 m suggests a slightly longer,

- **Fuelling and heat.** Distance is fine (the sub-5 km cap has expired), so the only
  thing to keep watching is hydration and pre-run carbohydrate, given that
  hypoglycemia and heat contributed to the June event.
