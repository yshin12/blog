The second half was 64 s/mi faster than the first. Miles 1–8 averaged 8:54 /mi (5:32 /km) at 127 bpm, and miles 9–16 averaged 7:50 /mi (4:52 /km) at 142 bpm. That second-half pace is exactly the fast end of the endurance band. The heart rate stayed at 144 or below through mile 15, and cadence (171 → 189 spm) and stride length (0.98 → 1.20 m) both increased as the run went on. That means your form got better as you tired, not worse. Mile 16 at 7:06 /mi (4:24 /km) was faster than endurance pace. It did no harm today, but on tired legs it puts extra load on the hamstring, so keep long-run finishes at 7:40–7:50 /mi until the race.
