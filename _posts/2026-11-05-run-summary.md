The five reps took **19:09 in total, an average of 3:50 /km**. That is in the middle of the VO2max band (3:45–3:55 /km, or 90–94 s per 400 m). The spread between the fastest and slowest rep was only 4 s, with a slight fade on the last two. Cadence on the reps was 194–198 spm with strides of about 1.28–1.31 m, so form held up. The strides were under 20 s each, a little quicker than the 20–22 s guide, which is fine for a set of four.

**HR note:** rep HR rose to 168 on rep 3, then fell to 162 and 156 while pace stayed at 3:50–3:51. That drop is about 12 bpm, smaller than the 20+ bpm step-downs seen on Oct 31, Jun 6 and Apr 18. It is still the same direction. It should not be read as the effort getting easier. Add this trace to the ones going to the cardiologist.

This was the first structured VO2max session since the Oct 11 half marathon. The rep pacing was close to textbook, and the warm-up and cooldown were both easy enough to frame the work properly. With Wednesday's rest day, the week stands at **32.58 km** (Mon 6.10, Tue 14.16, Wed 0, Thu 12.32).

**Left hamstring/glute:** a 5 × 1 km session at 3:50 /km on a flat track is a moderate load for the area. Rep cadence and stride stayed steady to the end, so there is no sign of guarding in the data. If there is any soreness at 2/10 or more tomorrow morning, keep Friday to an easy recovery run and do isometric bridges or holds rather than stretching.
