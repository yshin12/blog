## Notes

- **PHT (9 days post-flare):** This is the first faster-paced work since the 2026-05-10 glute flare. Three short reps at HM-pace-ish effort is a sensible re-entry — short enough to avoid loading the proximal tendon under fatigue. Monitor the next 24-48 h for any glute tightness; if quiet, the next quality session can extend to 4-5 reps or longer reps. Continue isometric holds, avoid aggressive hamstring stretching.
- Cadence at 174.6 spm is slightly under the usual 180+ — likely because the warm-up/cooldown share dominated the average. The fast reps showed 191-194 spm, which is excellent.
- Good discipline on the cooldown (2+ km of true easy running) — keeps the session aerobic-leaning despite the speed work.

Solid, controlled return to intensity. 👍
