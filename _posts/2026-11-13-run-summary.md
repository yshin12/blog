This was the easiest run of the month. HR averaged 110 and never went above 120, well under the recovery ceiling (76% of max, about 134 bpm). The HR range for the whole 51 minutes was only 10 beats. The pace was much slower than the usual recovery band (5:25–5:55 /km), which is fine for a rest-day jog. Cadence dropped a little to 174 spm, as you'd expect at very slow speeds. The working plan called for 8–12 km easy, flat, with no strides. The run came in shorter and slower than that, but it matched the intent: easy, flat, nothing fast.

**Left hamstring/glute:** flat, slow and steady with no strides, so this is about as little tendon load as running gets. Nothing in the data points to strain. Saturday's morning checks (bed, chair, single-leg bridge, stairs) decide whether strides go back in. Hills stay off until about Nov 22.
