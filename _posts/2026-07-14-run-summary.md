### ⚠️ This session runs against current medical advice

Since the **2026-06-06 race collapse and hospitalization** (troponin peaked >500, ALT peaked >1000), the standing instruction has been: **every run easy, conversational, low aerobic HR — no intervals, tempo, threshold, progression finishes, or racing until cardiology clears harder work.** The distance cap was lifted; the intensity restriction was **not**.

This session was four hard reps at 4:56–5:10 /km with HR reaching **169 bpm** and drifting to 163 avg on the last rep — squarely in the territory that's currently off the table. This is worth naming plainly rather than praising the splits.

**Two things to sort out:**
1. **Has the stress echocardiogram (early July) come back, and did it clear you for intensity?** If yes — great, and this session is a reasonable reintroduction. If it hasn't been done or reviewed, hard efforts should wait.
2. **GI and Cardiology follow-ups** are still pending. Those appointments should gate the return to speed work, not the calendar.

### If you are cleared

The execution itself was sound: reps held 4:56–5:10 with good consistency, cadence stayed at 179 spm, and the 3.3 km warmup plus 1.6 km cooldown were properly done. The HR spike on rep 4 (163 avg vs. 147–151 on reps 1–3) is a normal accumulation, but it's the metric to watch — if HR climbs disproportionately to pace on later reps, end the session there.

### If you are not yet cleared

Back to easy-only until the appointments happen. Hydration, fueling (avoid hypoglycemia), and heat management remain the priorities in July. One session won't undo the recovery, but a pattern of them could.

Please confirm the stress echo result before the next hard day.
