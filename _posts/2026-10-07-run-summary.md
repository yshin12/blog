### Notes

- **Good:** the pace was very even, with every mile between 8:50 and 8:59 /mi. HR averaged 127, about 72% of max, which is well inside the recovery zone. Recovery HR at this pace is back to your normal range the day after hard running, which is a good sign that the post-flu recovery is on track.
- **Watch:** HR went up to 130–131 over miles 3–6 and peaked at 138. That is close to the 133 limit, so it doesn't leave much margin. Run tomorrow a little slower, around 9:05 /mi (5:39 /km), to bank freshness.
- **Hamstring/glute and groin:** cadence (172–175) and stride length (1.02–1.04 m) stayed steady across all the splits. There's nothing in the data that suggests guarding or a shortened stride on one side. Do the pillow-squeeze check for the groin again tomorrow morning, and keep doing the isometric bridge holds through Thursday.
