### Notes

A **new half marathon PB** of **1:28:19** — a clean ~3:40 improvement over the previous 1:32 best, and the first HM since the proximal hamstring tendinopathy diagnosis in early March. The race executed almost exactly to plan: a controlled opening 5 km in the low 4:10s, a steady mid-race block at 4:09–4:14/km, and a confident progression in the final third with the closing kilometers landing at 3:50, 3:58, and 3:59/km. Avg HR of 158 bpm with a max of only 170 suggests there was still aerobic headroom — a good sign that fitness is genuinely above this pace and the conservative pacing worked.

Cadence held at 185 spm throughout, climbing to 188–192 in the closing surges, which kept ground contact short and protected the hamstring tendon from long-lever eccentric loads. PHT stayed quiet. The 101 m gain / 194 m loss profile suggests a net-downhill course that helped, but the pacing discipline and the negative-split shape (4:11 → 4:08 average over halves) are the real story. Recovery walk + easy spin tomorrow, no running for 2–3 days.
