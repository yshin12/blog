**Run type: LT (lactate threshold)** — a classic Pfitzinger two-interval tempo session, with
a strides primer between warm-up and the first rep.

### Shape of the run

**Warm-up — 3.22 km (2.00 mi), 16:59 @ 5:16/km (8:29/mi).** HR climbed gently from 107 to
134 bpm. A properly progressive warm-up: 5:39/km down to 5:07/km over eight quarter-miles.

**Strides — 3 × ~100 m.** 19.6 s, 20.3 s and 20.4 s, at roughly 3:12, 3:19 and 3:28 /km
(5:09, 5:20, 5:35 /mi), with 48–54 s jog recoveries. Cadence spiked to 196–203 spm. This is
textbook execution — the target for a 100 m stride is 20–22 s, and all three landed inside
it. Priming the neuromuscular system before a threshold effort is exactly what these are for.

**LT interval 1 — 4.83 km (3.00 mi) in 20:13 @ 4:11/km (6:44/mi).** Target band is
4:05–4:12/km (6:34–6:45/mi). This sat right at the slower edge of the band and barely moved:
the twelve quarter-mile splits ran 6:52, 6:44, 6:39, 6:45, 6:49, 6:45, 6:43, 6:43, 6:41,
6:41, 6:44, 6:41 /mi. After the first split settled in, ten of the remaining eleven fell
inside a 6-second window. HR rose 136 → 169, averaging 162 bpm (~88% of max).

**Recovery — 0.64 km (0.40 mi) in 3:51.** Plan called for a 4-minute jog. Close enough that
the difference is noise.

**LT interval 2 — 4.02 km (2.50 mi) in 17:14 @ 4:17/km (6:54/mi).** Ten splits: 6:45, 6:42,
6:49, 6:48, 6:56, 6:54, 7:01, 7:02, 6:59, 6:56 /mi. Started inside the band and drifted
about 6 s/km slower than interval 1, with HR averaging 167 bpm and topping out at 171
(~91% of max — the ceiling of the threshold range).

**Cool-down — 2.64 km (1.64 mi) @ 6:00/km (9:40/mi).** HR fell back to 137–146 bpm.

### Plan adherence

That is about as close to a plan as a session gets. This was also a genuine step up from last
week's 18 + 14 — 37 minutes of threshold work versus 32, and it was absorbed without the
wheels coming off.

The second interval running 6 s/km slower deserves a fair reading rather than a critical one.
It was run in 26–28°C after 20 minutes of threshold work was already in the legs, and HR was
pinned at 91% of max — the effort was fully there, the heat just took the pace. Cardiac drift
of 5 bpm between the two blocks at nearly identical effort is a heat signature, not a fitness
gap. On a cool day these would likely both sit at 4:11/km.

### Hamstring / glute note

This was the highest-risk session on the calendar for the tendon, and the data looks clean.
Cadence stayed high and stable through both intervals (187–196 spm) and stride length held at
115–124 cm without collapsing late in interval 2 — a guarding hamstring usually shows up as
shortening stride with rising cadence, and that pattern is absent. The strides at 3:12/km
also passed without incident, which is meaningful, since near-sprint pace is where a proximal
hamstring complains first.

That said: this is roughly 53 km across three days on a tendon that only began complaining a
week ago, and today added a hard threshold load on top. **The real test is tomorrow morning.**
If the sit-bone area reads 2/10 or worse on waking, or is stiff for more than a few minutes,
trim Thursday's endurance run. Keep the isometric work going — long-hold bridges, single-leg
holds — and keep leaving static hamstring stretching alone.

### Heat note

Average 25.9°C with the warm-up starting at 28°C, and 1,199 calories burned in 78 minutes.
Given the June history, this is worth naming plainly: replace fluids and sodium properly
tonight, and get carbohydrate in soon after a session of this intensity. Hot-weather
threshold work is where the margin gets thin, so front-load hydration before tomorrow rather
than catching up during it.

### What's next

Thursday is **endurance, 18 km (11 mi)** at 4:39–5:05/km (7:30–8:10/mi) — though after
today's threshold effort and three consecutive days of running, the slower end of that band,
or even recovery pace, is the smarter read. Friday is rest.

### Takeaway

The plan asked for 20 + 16 minutes at threshold and got 20 + 17, at the right paces, in
uncomfortable heat, with the strides executed to spec and no sign of hamstring trouble in the
numbers. This was the session the whole week was built around, and it landed.
